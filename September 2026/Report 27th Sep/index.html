Supervised by: Mr. Gichuhi & Ken
Course Engineering, Green Construction, Dam Liners & Material Logistics

Field operations on Sunday, 27th September 2026 progressed smoothly under Mr. Gichuhi and Ken, focusing on putting green sub-base construction, earthwork shaping, dam liner setup, material deliveries, and utility piping:

  • Green 7 Hardcore Placement & Red Soil Application: Site workers placed and broke down hardcore rocks directly over the clean ballast stone layer that encases the subgrade PVC drainage pipes and herringbone trenches. Following the rock layer placement, red soil was fully applied and leveled across the entire green bed and apron perimeter, bringing Green 7 into its proper structural shape.
  • Hardcore Haulage with Pickup: Mr. Gichuhi's pickup truck was utilized to haul loads of hardcore rocks from the stockpiles at Green 1 over to Green 7, supplying the necessary rock material directly to the green site for manual placement.
  • Green 18 Reshaping & Resizing: The site crew worked on Green 18, reshaping the contours and resizing the playing platform using red soil fill and edge grading to align with design standards.
  • Dam 1 Liner Installation: Technicians and workers advanced into the final phase of setting up the heavy-duty polythene dam liner across the floor and embankment slopes of Dam 1, securing edges and finalizing basin coverage.
  • Dam 3 Liner Connection: The team interconnected the dam liners that were previously laid out along the channel of Dam 3, joining and sealing overlapping sheets to establish a unified watertight membrane.
  • Material Deliveries: Material logistics brought in 1 lorry load of clean river sand and 1 lorry load of volcanic pumice aggregate, both staged on site for upcoming green foundation and sub-base works.
  • Drainage & Water Piping to Maina's: Plumbing and drainage workers installed water and drainage pipework along the perimeter trench line, extending the pipeline run all the way up to Maina's place.
