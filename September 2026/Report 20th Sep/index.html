  • Dam 2 Embankment Construction & Liner Preparation: Heavy earthmoving focused on Dam 2 embankment construction.   • Reshaping Both Number 7 Teebox Platforms: The XGMA backhoe loader completely reshaped and precision-leveled both platforms of Teebox 7 along the perimeter boundary fence line, reinforcing the elevated launch plateaus and sculpting protective shoulder berms.
  • Reshaping Teebox 16: Ground grading and elevation profiling were executed on Teebox 16, establishing a smooth, level launch platform aligned with the back-nine fairway playing corridor.
  • Reshaping Green 15 & Green 10 Boundary: Machine and manual grading sculpted the raised soil boundary berm separating Green 15 and Green 10, establishing clean perimeter definition, apron transitions, and water runoff contours.
  • Compacting Volcanic Pumice Sub-Base on Greens 10 – 18: Mechanical compaction was performed across the volcanic pumice aggregate beds on Greens 10 through 18 using a walk-behind vibratory roller compactor to lock the sub-base profile into final design grade.
  • Joe's Crew Pumice Leveling & Backfilling: Working alongside the vibratory roller, Joe's crew utilized shovels and wheelbarrows to backfill unlevelled hollows and low spots with volcanic pumice aggregate during compaction, ensuring an even, uniform foundation across the putting greens.
