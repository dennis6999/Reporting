Sunday operations at Mucheru World of Golf on 4th October 2026 were highlighted by the presence of course designer Joe Kamau, Peter Githinji, and Carol on site, working closely with site supervisors Mr. Gichuhi and Ken to execute key earthwork shaping, green profiling, and fairway drainage design:

Hole 7 Architectural Milestone & Infrastructure Progress

Both playing platforms for Teebox 7 successfully reshaped and leveled; Green 7 putting surface contour-shaped with additional volcanic pumice; PVC pipe requirements surveyed and fairway drainage trench lines marked out; 1 truckload of washed golden river sand received; and Green 5 grass actively irrigated with automated sprinklers.

  • Joe Kamau, Peter Githinji & Carol Technical Oversight: Golf course designer Joe Kamau, accompanied by Peter Githinji and Carol, conducted comprehensive on-site inspections and active field coordination with Mr. Gichuhi and Ken, personally guiding the contour shaping of Green 7, aligning the dual platforms of Tee 7, and establishing drainage pipe elevations.
  • Reshaping Both Number 7 Teeboxes: Field crews utilizing hand tools (jembes, hoes, and shovels) and earthmoving equipment actively reshaped both the upper and lower launch platforms for Hole 7. Workers excavated, backfilled, and precision-leveled the red soil platforms, expanding the teeing surfaces and creating clean, flat launch terraces aligned along the perimeter boundary fence and relocated palm trees.

Under Joe Kamau's direct supervision, the team rake-leveled the pumice layer to sculpt designed putting undulations, gentle runoff slopes, and defined perimeter edges against the red soil surrounds.
  • PVC Pipe Measuring & Drainage Trench Marking: The technical survey team walked the playing corridors, utilizing measuring tapes to accurately calculate PVC pipe lengths required for fairway storm drainage. They clearly marked the ground alignment paths for excavation of drainage trenches designed to convey runoff safely toward course discharge points.
  • Fresh Delivery of River Sand: Material haulage logistics delivered 1 tipper lorry load of clean, washed golden river sand to the course. The sand was offloaded and stockpiled in an accessible staging area, ready for wheeling and spreading as the top agronomic rooting layer over prepared pumice green beds.
  • Turf Sprinkler Irrigation on Green 5: Overhead popup and rotor turf sprinklers were turned on across Putting Green 5, delivering fine, uniform water coverage across the 100% grass-established turf surface to nurture deep root development and sustain vibrant green turf health.
