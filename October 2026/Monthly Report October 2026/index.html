Total milk harvested across the 31 days was 227.5 Litres (averaging 7.3L/day, peaking at 9.0L/day). Commercial sales were 194.0 Litres, and internal farm allocations were 33.5 Litres. Production and sales are 100% balanced with zero discrepancy.

  • Estate Drainage & Compound Maintenance: Farm plumbers repaired and cleaned the wastewater pipes at the main house and installed protective cleanout fittings. Margaret and Monica kept the compound, lawns, and gardens clean.

🏡 Structural Milestone: Staff Quarters Concrete Slab Successfully Cast

Formwork timber supports were reinforced, high-strength steel bars were tied across the deck, electrical conduits were laid with junction boxes, and fresh concrete was poured and vibrated to complete the SQ roof slab.

  • Staff Quarters (SQ) Roof Slab Poured: Builders assembled timber support props, tied heavy steel reinforcement cages for drop beams, and laid rebar mesh across the deck.   • Roofing Maintenance & Gutter Clearance: Workers climbed the terracotta tile roofs of the main house, carport canopy, and generator shed to clear fallen leaves and inspect gutter drainage.
  • Water Tank & Stormwater Drainage: Steel reinforcement bars were assembled for the main water tank foundation slab, and stone retaining walls along the forest path were reinforced.
