Mucheru World of Golf Operations Summary

Riversand was applied and completely leveled across Green 7; riversand spreading commenced on Green 18, reaching 50% coverage over the pumice foundation; drainage trenches were dug for green and teebox inlets and outlets; the farm tractor hauled PVC pipes from the greenhouse; crew connected PVC pipes in trenches; Hunter automatic solenoid valves arrived for all greens; and active water replenishment continued into Dams 1, 2, and 3.

  • Riversand Spreading & Precision Leveling on Green 7: Following initial delivery, wheelbarrow teams under Joe Kamau's supervision transported golden riversand across Green 7. Ground crews using leveling rakes and straightedges spread and leveled the sand layer uniformly over the volcanic pumice bed to establish the finished agronomic growing profile.

  • Delivery of Hunter Solenoid Valves for All Greens: A complete consignment of heavy-duty Hunter automatic solenoid valves was delivered on site. These electronic valves will be installed across all greens to enable automated on/off control of irrigation water flow through the sprinkler network.
  • PVC Pipe Haulage by Farm Tractor & Trench Connection: The farm tractor and red cargo trailer hauled multiple loads of orange PVC drainage pipes from greenhouse storage across to the golf site. Plumbing crews laid the pipes into the excavated trenches, aligning and jointing connections to ensure uninterrupted gravity flow.
  • Continuous Water Inflow into Dams 1, 2 & 3: Water inlet feeder channels and pipelines discharging into Dam 1, Dam 2, and Dam 3 operated continuously, sustaining active water storage replenishment across the property's reservoir system.
