Operations at Mucheru World of Golf on Sunday, 11th October 2026 registered major agronomic, civil drainage, and irrigation layout achievements under the site supervision of Mr. Gichuhi and Ken, with technical leadership and agronomy execution led by Joe Kamau and Carol alongside Peter Githinji's trenching crew:

Mucheru World of Golf Operations Summary

Golden riversand and surplus red soil were leveled across Greens 2, 3, 4, 8, and 9; pop-up sprinkler positions were accurately measured and marked across every green except Green 5; on-site plumber cut and joined orange PVC drainage pipes along excavated network trenches; Githinji's crew dug incoming water drainage trenches for greens and teeboxes; and continuous water intake into Dam 3 progressed steadily.

  • Riversand Spreading & Precision Leveling (Greens 2, 3, 4, 8 & 9): Joe Kamau's agronomy team spread and leveled golden riversand over the volcanic pumice beds on Greens 8, 4, 9, 3, and 2. Ground crews used landscape rakes and straightedges to achieve uniform agronomic rootzone thickness and smooth surface contours across all five greens.
  • Leveling of Surplus Red Soil Surrounds (Greens 2, 3, 4, 8 & 9): Alongside sand leveling, field teams worked around the perimeters of Greens 8, 4, 9, 3, and 2 to grade and level the surplus red topsoil previously delivered and placed along collars, aprons, and outer embankments, creating clean transitions between putting surfaces and surrounds.
  • Pop-Up Sprinkler Staking & Measurement (17 Greens Marked): Joe Kamau systematically surveyed, measured with tape, and marked the precise installation locations for automatic pop-up sprinklers across 17 putting greens (covering every green across the course with the sole exception of Green 5). This layout ensures uniform 360-degree irrigation coverage once pipe connections are completed.
  • Drainage PVC Pipe Jointing & Plumbing Connections: The on-site plumber worked directly along excavated network trenches, cutting orange PVC pipes to length with a handsaw and joining pipe segments to convey excess runoff and stormwater away from greens and fairway corridors.
  • Green & Teebox Inlet Drainage Trenching (Githinji's Crew): Peter Githinji's crew conducted manual pick and shovel excavation of dedicated inlet water drainage trenches servicing both putting greens and championship teeboxes to manage gravity inflows.
  • Continuous Water Intake Progress at Dam 3: Replenishment of Dam 3 progressed smoothly throughout the day, with water flowing steadily through inlet channels and accumulating within the lined reservoir basin.
