Apron Pipe Leveling, Drainage Invert Shaping & Multi-Reservoir Inflow Brief

Field civil, drainage, and irrigation operations across Mucheru World of Golf on Wednesday, 4th November 2026 focused on apron pipe realignment, localized green and boundary trench grading, teebox site clearing, and ongoing dam replenishment under the supervision of Mr. Gichuhi and Willy:

Golf Course Operational Highlights

Irrigation crews continued systematic excavation along putting green aprons to expose buried HDPE sprinkler pipes, realigning them to the proper depth flush with the finished apron surface before backfilling. Extensive earthwork continued along course boundaries, with laborers shaping the main perimeter drainage trench and grading the external drainage trench outside the golf boundary fence for positive gravity flow. Surface water inflows continued steadily into all three reservoirs (Dam 1, Dam 2, and Dam 3).

  • Irrigation HDPE Pipe Realignment on Green Aprons: Crews excavated along green perimeters to adjust HDPE sprinkler pipes flush with the surrounding apron grade before refilling trenches with red soil.
  • Teebox 12 Rock & Waste Clearance: Laborers removed scattered rocks, excess earth, and unwanted waste around both teebox platforms at Hole 12, leaving clean platform borders.
  • Green 6 Drainage Trench Excavation & Grading: Field workers deepened, shaped, and leveled the drainage trench adjacent to Green 6 to establish smooth gravity water runoff.
  • Main Perimeter Drainage Trench Leveling: Workforce teams graded the trench floor and trimmed sidewalls along the inner perimeter drainage line to maintain continuous gravity waterflow.
  • External Perimeter Drainage Channel Shaping: Laborers worked along the trench corridor outside the golf site fence, shaping the invert bed to support smooth discharge of surface runoff.
  • Continuous Inflow into Dams 1, 2, and 3: Surface runoff and inflow pipes delivered continuous water into Dam 1, Dam 2, and Dam 3, raising water storage levels across all lined basins.
