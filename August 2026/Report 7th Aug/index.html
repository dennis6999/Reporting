1. Golf Project (Mucheru World of Golf)

Dam excavation commenced, Green No. 2 topsoil removal, and continued site clearance

Overseen by: Mr. Gichuhi & Ken
🏗️ Dam Excavation
  • Dam Excavation Commenced: Excavation of the dam began at 9:00 AM, marking the start of a key infrastructure phase for the golf course water management system.
  • Soil Dumping on Tee Boxes: Excavated soil was transported and strategically dumped on Tee Box 5, 6, 8, and 9 for grade build-up and terrain shaping.

⚠️ Equipment Breakdown Notice

One of the trucks experienced a mechanical breakdown during operations. The remaining excavation and soil transport work was completed using a single truck for the rest of the day.
