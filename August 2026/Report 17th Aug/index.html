🌱 Greens 1 & 3 Polythene Membrane Installation & Red Soil Levelling
  • Polythene Membrane Liner Installation: Heavy-duty impermeable black polythene membrane was carefully measured, cut, and laid along the sub-base perimeter of Green No. 1 and Green No. 3 over the hardcore stone bed to establish clean hydraulic containment.
  • Red Soil Spreading & Perimeter Locking: Teams deployed wheelbarrows and shovels to distribute rich red topsoil immediately over the polythene liner margins, securing the edges firmly in place.
  • Red Soil Surface Levelling: Systematic manual levelling and rough grading conducted across the red soil layer on Greens 1 and 3 to build up the required putting surface sub-grade elevation.

🎯 Greens Foundation Milestone: Greens 1 & 3 Membrane Lining Complete

Polythene membrane positioning and red soil base spreading have been successfully completed on Greens 1 and 3, setting the stage for final rootzone contouring.

📋 Land Acquisition Follow-Up Meeting & Timber Posts Relocation
  • Land Acquisition Follow-Up Meeting: A strategic follow-up consultation regarding ongoing land acquisition was hosted on-site at the property today to align on boundary surveys, documentation, and expansion plans.
  • Relocation of Tree Posts at Golf Site (Overseen by Kinoti): Dedicated logistics crews under Kinoti's supervision loaded felled tree posts, timber logs, and branches onto tractor trailers, transporting and neatly stacking them at designated staging points across the golf site for property boundary fencing.
