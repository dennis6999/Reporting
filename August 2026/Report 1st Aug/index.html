1. Golf Project (Mucheru World of Golf)

Construction, deliveries, site visits, and architectural marking

Overseen by: Ken
🏗️ Construction & Groundwork Progress

All activities were carried out with 10 workers across Green 9 and Green 4.

  • Levelling Hardcore: The hardcore base was carefully leveled to ensure a uniform and stable foundation across the green construction area.
  • Installing PVC Pipes: PVC drainage and irrigation pipes were measured, cut, and installed according to the site layout plan.
  • Applying Ballast: A layer of ballast was applied over the prepared surface to strengthen the sub-base and improve drainage performance.
  • Sand Delivery: A sand truck delivery arrived on site. The sand was received and offloaded for upcoming construction and layering works.
  • Pumice Delivery: One full lorry of pumice was delivered to the site for use in upcoming green layering.
📐 Site Visit & Architectural Works
  • Site Visit: Joe Kamau and Caroline visited the project site during the day to review progress and provide guidance.
  • Clubhouse Marking: Architect Kevin Njoroge attended the site and marked out the clubhouse location and dimensions as per the approved plans.
